# Sci-Fi Viticulture & Sommelier Phrasing Cheat Sheet

### 1. Origin & Cultivation (Where & How It Grew)

* **"Estate-bottled under..."** *(e.g., "...the thermal shields of eastern Hellas Basin.")*
* **"Cultivated in pressurized sub-surface vaults..."** *(indicates radiation-shielded deep-ground growing).*
* **"Sourced from third-generation cuttings rooted in..."** *(replaces standard Earth "old vine" pedigree).*
* **"Tended in low-yield bio-tiers along the rift wall..."** *(implies high-density, high-care farming).*
* **"Canopy-managed under narrow-band solar arrays..."** *(replaces natural sunlight descriptions).*

---

### 2. Soil, Substrate & Water Chemistry (Terroir)

* **"Rooted in de-salted basaltic regolith amended with..."** *(clay, calcium carbonate, or organic slurry).*
* **"Fed via closed-loop mineral hydrology..."** *(describes drip/hydro-irrigation).*
* **"Grown in deep synthetic loams calibrated to..."** *(Bordeaux clay / Côte de Nuits limestone equivalents).*
* **"Stressed by controlled nitrogen starvation post-veraison..."** *(explains concentration of grape flavors).*

---

### 3. Climate & Atmospheric Simulation

* **"Paced to a 28-day simulated Mediterranean diurnal cycle..."** *(day/night temperature shift).*
* **"Shielded beneath polarized radiation baffles..."** *(filters out cosmic rays/UV spikes).*
* **"Subjected to chilled atmospheric scrub cycles..."** *(preserves crisp natural acidity at 'night').*
* **"Thermal gradient managed via geothermal tap..."** *(ambient rootbed warmth).*

---

### 4. Harvest, Fermentation & Aging

* **"Cold-macerated at 0.38G..."** *(or 0.04G on Titania / micro-G in transit).*
* **"Spontaneous native-yeast fermentation in sealed titanium vats..."**
* **"Racked into toasted Earth-import French oak..."** *(signals massive luxury/expense).*
* **"Station-aged along the non-spinning central spindle..."** *(zero-G lees contact).*
* **"Vinted in transit across the Martian opposition run..."** *(orbital aging).*

---

### 5. Critical / Technical Snob Phrasing (Mel's Voice)

* **"The acidity is totally blown..."** *(flat, over-heated fruit).*
* **"You can taste the scrubber scrub..."** *(chemical/synthetic residue from air filters).*
* **"Forced veraison..."** *(growers blasted light/heat to rush harvest, ruining tannins).*
* **"A flabby mid-palate with an artificial alcohol spike..."**
* **"There's a phantom aluminum note cutting right through the finish..."** *(her core anomaly).*
