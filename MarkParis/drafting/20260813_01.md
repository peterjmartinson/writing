Mel Navarro was running out of time.  She was also standing still.  The lanky barista was taking her time.  Mel's time.

_What is it with these Outers?_

As she drummed her fingers on the counter, waiting for her Americano, her attention suddenly latched onto the two boys sitting a few feet away.  She didn't allow herself to look over at them, because she was currently using her eyes to force coffee girl to move faster.

"Where did I put that thing?"

"Wey, what are you looking for?"

"The adapter."

Real poetry out here, thought Mel.  She stared harder at the girl, just starting to put hot water in the cup.

"What?"

"The adapter.  You know, for my headphones."

"Slushai, Wey, why don't you just get the cordless ones?"

"Sound quality is shit.  Where did I put that thing?"

Mel had hoped to pop in this wretched establishment, get a quick cup of whatever was already brewed, and be on her way.  Of course, the last coffee shop for about 150,000,000 kilometers doesn't brew pots of coffee.

"Maybe it's in Uranus," quipped the friend.

Mel had had enough.  She redirected her gaze at these two infants and snapped, "It's _ooo-RAH-nus_, you idiots."

Just then the barista put the coffee down in front of Mel.  "Uh, Bell?"

Mel snatched the coffee and stormed out.  She barely noticed the two spacer Nacos coming in, smelling of grease and ozone.  Her boots rang out like hammer blows as she marched down the spine of Elsinore's outer ring, heading for Bay 13.  She was about to be late for her first meeting with Rukai Long, captain of the ice mining vessel _Ozymandias_.

Mel did not like to be late, and it was very rare for her to be so.

She took a sip of her coffee.

"This tastes like shit."

Mel was on the job, as quality control specialist for Consolidated Wine and Spirits, Inc.  She loved her job.  And she was good at it.  Who else gets to travel while tasting all the wines the Solar System had to offer?  And get paid for it!

But there was something wrong about this most recent batch of Cabernet from the Peacock Vintners.  The grapes had been cultivated on the east flank of Pavonis Mons, Tharsis region, rooted in local basaltic regolith amended with clay from the floor of Valles Marineris.  They even racked the wine in Earth-imported French oak barrels.  It should have tasted like wild blackberry and dried bay leaf over wet gravel, with the oak completely integrated into the mid-palate.

And it did.  But it also had a phantom note of aluminum.  Enough so that it almost turned Mel's stomach.

"Maybe that was a bad bottle.  Open another one."

Her assistant Carletta did as she was told, and poured a tiny bit into a clean wine glass.  Mel took a sip.

"Blech!"

Curious, Carletta tried some herself.  She wasn't gifted like Mel, but she had trained along with the best in Bordeaux.  She knew how to evaluate a wine.  To her, it tasted like she expected in an expert vintner's product.

"It tastes fine to me, Mel."

"You don't taste that metallic note cutting right through the finish?"

Carletta shook her head.

"This stuff is bad," Mel declared.  "We can't accept it."

That set off a chain of fateful actions that almost cost Mel her position.  Carletta did sign off on the denial of this batch from Peacock, but made a note that she thought it tasted fine.  This note ran all the way up the ladder to VP of Portfolio Sandra Gupta-Gupta.  Sheila tested the Cabernet herself, and declared it perfect.

All the way up that ladder, and Mel hit banged her head on each rung.  Every step of the way, she argued her case that the batch was not drinkable, while various sensory panels said she was wrong.
