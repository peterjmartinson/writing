Waiting in the coffee shop for Windy and Alex to show up, Mark turned his attention to the people.

Everyone here was younger than himself.  The barista was probably in her early 20's.  The two kids sitting over in the corner were probably teenagers.  One was earphones deep in his terminal, the other pulling booklike things out of a formless brown bag.  The guy by the windows hunched over his glowing screen, also probably in his 20's.  All four of them were pushing 7 feet tall.

The customer drumming her fingers on the plexiglass counter was not pushing 7 feet.  "Must be from Earth," Mark thought.  She had straight black hair pulled back in a taut pony tail, gray pants, and was wearing some kind of burgundy tunic.

Grumble!" "Spit!" "Hiss!" went the coffee rig. A regular americano. The lanky barista may look dense, but Mark knew she could pull a tight ristretto on command. Which meant drowning a roast like Miranda dark in hot water was tunic lady's own idea.  Amateur.

Headphone kid at one point pulled one side off and started talking to his ever more frantic, bag searching friend.  Mark couldn't make out their dialogue with all the espresso pulling, but it looked like he wasn't helping.

The customer suddenly looked over at them, barked something that sounded like "ooo-aaah" at them, and went back to drumming her fingers.  Headphone sheepishly covered his ears back up, and bag boy put all his shit back in the bag and opened his own terminal, sans headphones.

On her way out, tunic lady almost collided with Windy, who had Alex in tow.  Windy stopped, gave the lady a glare, and for a moment Mark was worried she would start throwing her gangster shit around.  Thankfully, tunic lady was gone before Windy could start.

Alex went to the counter to order, Windy came straight to Mark's table.

"Mark", Windy said, "You're fired."
