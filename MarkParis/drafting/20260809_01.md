After a few more pleasantries, or unpleasantries, Windy and Alejandro left Mark in the coffee shop with all his anxiety.  He felt all the eyes on him.  

When he finally looked up from his empty cup, he saw nobody was paying any attention to him.  Except the barista.

Mark stood up, went to the counter, and ordered one more cup of Miranda.  Just regular brew.  The empathetic barista drew it in his cup, gave it to him, and said, "on the house, gyre.  Sucks about your job."

"Yeah, thanks," Mark said.  He took his coffee and walked out into the station, aiming vaguely for his lodging.

Elsinore Base was not highly populated by people, though there were the ever present drones scurrying about, cleaning things and doing whatever robots do when they're around humans.  The people who were here were dominated by the hard rock type, who survey and direct mining operations on the far moons and other objects out past Uranus.  Not a job for the cowardly, since space miners are known for getting in close to the action.  Though safety is treated seriously out here, sometimes you have to get human eyes on the rock in order to sort out what is what.

Mark walked by a few of these miners, and a few smaller people who were probably their crew.  Probably, a jalopy was getting its overhaul here, prepping to head back out towards Neptune or further out, looking for wealth to bring glory to the company of the day.
