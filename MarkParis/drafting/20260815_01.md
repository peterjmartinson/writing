Sandra Gupta-Gupta

Mel hit her head on every rung up that ladder.

"Mel what are you doing," Sandra asked.  "You and I both know this wine tastes great.  And Peacock is a valuable customer."

Mel thought about how to answer.  But not too long.  "I am not crazy, Sandra, and I don't have a vendetta against Peacock Vintners.  Their product is usually stellar, but this batch is off."  Mel recited the lines she had said at least a dozen times before.  "It has a metallic note that cuts through right after the finish."

Sandra was having none of it though.  "Mel, you have been at this company for 13 years, and have been a valuable asset.  However, Peacock has been a customer for almost 30.  We can't just go back to them and tell them they have a shitty batch."

"I'm not saying they are bad vintners.  But this batch tastes awful.  We can't sell it and expect people to trust us afterward."

Sandra looked at her hard.  "Nobody else here can taste this 'metallic tang' you say is there.  Why do you think that is?"

This fucking bitch, thought Mel.
