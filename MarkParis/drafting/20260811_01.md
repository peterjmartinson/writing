Mark walked into the elevator that headed back to the hub.  There was another guy in there.  By the tight pants, bright orange jacket, and sharp haircut, obviously a gas miner.  He backed away a bit from Mark as he got in, looked like he was about to make a break for it.

"You're Paris."

"Yeah," Mark said, "Didn't realize I was so well known.  Don't worry, I won't blow the airlock or anything."

Gas Miner relaxed a little bit, but still kept a wary eye on Mark as the door slid shut.  He quickly scanned the edges of the door, probably for areas of potential breach.

"Word travels fast here," he said, squinting a bit at one section of the door's rubber seal.
