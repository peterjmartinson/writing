Okay, here are the step-by-step instructions to create your survey in Google Forms, including how to randomize the order of the job ranking question:

Step 1: Go to Google Forms

    Open a web browser and go to https://docs.google.com/forms/.
    If you're not already logged in to your Google account, you'll be prompted to do so.

Step 2: Start a New Form

    On the Google Forms homepage, you'll see options to start a new form. Click the "+" (Blank) icon to create a new, empty form.

Step 3: Title Your Survey

    At the top left, where it says "Untitled form," click and enter a descriptive title for your survey (e.g., "Job Preference Survey").
    You can also add a brief description below the title if you like.

Step 4: Add the Introductory Text

    The first question will appear as "Untitled question." Instead of a question, we'll add your introductory text as a Description.

    Click the "+" icon on the right-hand sidebar to add a new element.

    From the dropdown menu at the top of this new element, select Title and description.

    In the "Title" field, you can leave it blank or add a short heading like "Welcome."

    In the "Description" field, copy and paste the introductory text we created:

    Thank you for taking the time to participate in this survey!

    This survey asks about your job preferences and some general background information. Your responses will help us understand career interests across different groups of people.

    Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability. Your responses will be kept confidential and used for research purposes only.

    The survey should take approximately [Estimate the time it will take, e.g., 5-10 minutes] to complete.

    Click "Next" to begin.

Step 5: Create the Job Ranking Question (and Enable Randomization)

    Click the "+" icon again to add your first actual question.

    Question Title: Enter something like "Please rank the following job categories in order of your preference, where 1 is your most preferred and 9 is your least preferred."

    Question Type: From the dropdown menu, select Linear scale.

    Scale: Set the scale from 1 to 9.

    Label (optional): You can label "1" as "Most Preferred" and "9" as "Least Preferred" to provide further guidance.

    Now, to list the job categories that need to be ranked, you will add another question. Click the "+" icon.

    Question Title: Enter "Rank the following job categories:"

    Question Type: Select Multiple choice grid.

    Rows: Enter each of your job categories as a separate row:
        Working in a factory that manufactures goods (Manufacturing)
        Software Development
        Information Security (Infosec)
        DevOps/Systems Administration
        Hospitality (e.g., hotels, tourism)
        Food Service (e.g., restaurants, cafes)
        Sales
        Farm/Agriculture
        Creative Industries (e.g., graphic design, marketing, media production)

    Columns: Enter the numbers 1 through 9 as the column options.

    Require a response in each row: Toggle this on. This will ensure participants rank all the job categories.

    To randomize the order of these job categories (rows):
        Click the three vertical dots (⋮) in the bottom right corner of this "Rank the following job categories" question.
        Select Shuffle row order. This will present the job categories in a random order for each participant.

Step 6: Add the Demographic Questions

    For each demographic question, click the "+" icon and choose the appropriate question type:
        Age Group: Dropdown or Multiple choice with your specified ranges (18-24, 25-34, etc.).
        Home State/Country: Short answer for free text input or Dropdown if you want to provide a list.
        Educational Status: Dropdown or Multiple choice with your specified options (High School Diploma, Associate's Degree, etc.).
        Gender Identity: Multiple choice with "Male," "Female," and "Other." For "Other," you might want to add a "Go to section based on answer" option to a short answer text field if they select "Other" for more detail (optional, depending on how detailed you need this).
        Primary Field of Study (if applicable): Short answer with a description like "If you have a college degree, please enter your primary field of study." You can also add a "Not applicable" option if using Multiple choice or Dropdown.
        Race or Ethnicity: Checkbox grid with the options we defined (White, Black or African American, Hispanic or Latino, Asian, etc., including "Some other race or ethnicity" with a short answer field, and "Prefer not to say"). Step 7: Add the Optional Open-Ended Question

    Click the "+" icon.
    Question Title: "What factors influence your job preferences?"
    Question Type: Select Paragraph for a longer free-text response.
    Mark this question as Not required by toggling the "Required" switch off.

Step 8: Review and Adjust Settings

    Click the gear icon (⚙️) at the top right to access the Settings.
    General: You can decide if you want to collect email addresses (not necessary for anonymous surveys but can be useful if you need to follow up). You can also limit responses to one per person if desired (requires respondents to sign in to Google).
    Presentation: We've already addressed shuffling question order for the ranking. You can also show a progress bar or confirmation message here.
    Quizzes: This section is for creating quizzes with scoring, which is not relevant to your survey.

Step 9: Preview Your Survey

    Click the eye icon (Preview) at the top right to see how your survey will look to participants. Test out the ranking and other questions.

Step 10: Send Your Survey

    Click the Send button at the top right.
    You'll see several options for sharing:
        Link (🔗): This is what you'll use to share directly with participants. You can copy the short URL for easier sharing.
        Email (✉️): You can email the survey directly to a list of email addresses.
        Embed (&lt;>): You can embed the survey into a website.

Important Considerations:

    Clarity: Ensure your question wording is clear and easy for participants to understand.
    Completeness: Make sure you've included all the necessary questions and options.
    Testing: Thoroughly test your survey before distributing it to a large number of participants to catch any errors or usability issues.

By following these steps, you should be able to create your job preference survey in Google Forms, including the randomization of the job ranking question. Good luck!
